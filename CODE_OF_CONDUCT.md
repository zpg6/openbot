# Code of conduct

OpenBot contributors must be able to discuss difficult technical work without personal attacks or intimidation.

## Expected conduct

- Critique code, design, evidence, and operational risk. Do not attack the author.
- State assumptions and show a reproduction when making a technical claim.
- Accept direct review without turning disagreement into a personal dispute.
- Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, and differences in experience.
- Keep private reports, credentials, personal data, and unpublished vulnerabilities private.
- Stop harassment, sexual attention, threats, doxxing, and discriminatory language immediately.

Strong review is welcome. Cruelty is not. A reviewer may call a change unsafe, incorrect, or unready and must explain why. Mocking the contributor adds nothing.

## Where this applies

This policy covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project chat, events, and private communication about OpenBot. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting conduct

Report conduct privately through a maintainer contact listed on the repository owner's profile. If no private contact is available, use the repository host's private vulnerability-reporting form and write `Conduct report` at the top. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue.

Maintainers will limit report access to people needed for review. They may remove content, lock a thread, issue a warning, require a pause, or ban participation. Retaliation against a reporter or witness violates this policy.

Maintainers must disclose a conflict and leave the decision to another maintainer when possible. If no unconflicted maintainer is available, the affected person should use the repository host's abuse-reporting process.
